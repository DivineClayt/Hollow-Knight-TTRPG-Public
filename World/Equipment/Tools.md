Tools are various gadgets you can find and craft with the resources scattered out in Hallownest, there is a variety you can create and find, and some are more useful than others in some situations. Some tools have a SOUL cost, alongside a limit as to how many you can carry. However there is a limit to how many tools in general you are able to carry, which is determined by your crest, which also determines what colour tools you can use and how many

Tools come in one of three colours: Red, Blue or Yellow.

Red tools are active tools, they cost a dice to use alongside some soul but they are usually pretty powerful and come with some additional effects when you attack an enemy with them over your primary weapon

Blue tools are passive tools, they do not cost dice to use, and they usually come with a decent benefit for yourself that just works on its own, sometimes certain conditions must be met before a passive tool can work, though its not often they have these conditions.

Yellow tools are navigation tools, they do not cost dice to use, and they typically help in exploration, such as pointing to the nearest mask shard or a general direction to help in where you should go next.
