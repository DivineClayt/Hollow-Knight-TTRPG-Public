#Core-Mechanics 
# Actions
When a person wants to do an action, they select one of their rolled dice and assign it to an action, which then happens instantly, any selected dice that have not been used cannot be converted to reactions at the end of the turn, so you must plan ahead for how many actions you think you will need to use.

---
# Reactions
During an opponents turn, if a person would be targeted by the opponent for an attack, you may use one of your dice in your reaction pool to dodge, you then roll this dice and compare the value to the opponents attack value, if your dice value is higher than the opponents, you successfully manage to dodge and depending on the type of attack, backstep out of the range, jump above the attack, or jump off a platform to go under the attack

Refer to [[Action Types]] for the actions and reactions you may perform on your turn and during the enemies turn
