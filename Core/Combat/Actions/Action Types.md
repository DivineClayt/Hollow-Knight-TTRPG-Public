There are multiple types of actions that you can perform during combat, as shown in the table below:


| Action Type | Effect                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| ----------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Walking     | Walk x squares equal to dice value                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| Jumping     | Jump (x+might) squares equal to dice value                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| Jump Move   | Jump (x+might) squares and move horizontally (x+might/2) squares equal to dice value                                                                                                                                                        |
| Attack      | Make an attack with your weapon in a cardinal direction                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| Special     | Use a unique action as part of a special ability                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| Intuition   | When this action is taken, add your intelligence to the dice value, if the value exceeds 8 (in some cases, a higher value), you can physically see on the grid what the enemy intends to do with its first action and the potential effects |
| Spell       | Use your SOUL to cast a spell you have learned, following its designated rules.                                                                                                                                                             |
| Heal        | Use your SOUL to mend your body, your specific roll is irrelevant heal 1 mask                                                                                                                                                               |

There is also two types of reactions you can perform during the enemy's turn if you're targeted for an attack:

| Reaction Type | Effect                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| ------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Dodging       | Take a D6 from your reaction pool and roll it, while adding your grace, if you roll higher than the total attack value, you dodge the attack, otherwise you get hit as normal. There might be some cases in which case you cannot dodge an attack, but it is rare.                                                                    |
| Parrying      | Take a D6 from your reaction pool and roll it, you may CHOOSE to add your grace to the roll, however you must determine this before rolling. If you roll exactly the same as the attack value, you parry the attack, otherwise you fail and take double damage, some attacks cannot be inherently parried, and you will be made aware |
When you successfully parry an attack, the following happens:

The enemy loses 2 dice from their dice pool for the next round, if the enemy has no dice left, they cannot act at all next round, being effectively stunned. For each dice the enemy would lose, if they do not have any left to lose, they instead take damage equal to the value of your dice used to parry

> Example: if you parried an enemy using a 5, and the enemy only had 1d6, they would lose that d6 and take 5 damage since they had no more dice left to lose.

When you fail to parry an attack, the following happens:

You lose 1 dice from your dice pool next round, you also take double damage, meaning an attack that would deal 1 mask of damage instead deals 2 masks of damage instead
