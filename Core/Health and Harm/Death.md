#Core-Mechanics 
There are some unfortunate times where your vessel may shatter and you might just break. However, death is not the end for you in this campaign, when you die, you are locked out of any ongoing fights, after the fight is over whether you won or lost, you re-materialize back at the last bench you rested at, and you leave behind a shade at the battlefield, a part of your soul has left your body, and you need it back.

When you have died, the "shaded" effect is applied to you, which has the following traits:

Shaded:
- Lose 1 Level, if this would make you lose a mask, you lose the mask temporarily. If you would die again, either you lose that level permanently and you go at risk of losing another, or you just lose another level and can still recollect all your lost levels when you kill it (DEBATING, UNSURE ON HOW HARSH TO BE)
- Your SOUL meter is capped to 2/3rds of what your max capacity is, refer to [[Health and SOUL]]
- When in the same room as your shade, you can kill it as a free action to regain all lost levels and repair your SOUL meter.
