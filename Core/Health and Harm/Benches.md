Benches are your checkpoints in the TTRPG, when you find a bench and sit, this is classed as a resting phase.

During your resting phase, the following takes place:

- Heal one mask for each die you choose to spend
- You may add or remove charms from your character, costs 1 dice
- You may spend any unspent ability points, no cost
- You may also craft an item provided you have the materials to craft them, costs 1 dice for basic items, 2 dice for complex items

Once you get up from a bench, you cannot rest at that bench again until you have rested at a different bench.

Benches are also not the only resting places, some areas may be comfortable enough to act as temporary rest, if this is the case, temporary rests only provide you 2 dice for your resting actions.
