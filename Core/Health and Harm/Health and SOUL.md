In this TTRPG, there are two core systems that are vital for you to know. Your health and your SOUL.

# Health

Your health deviates from traditional TTRPG, becoming Masks instead
![[Masks.png]]

At the start of the campaign, you will start with 5 masks, each time you are hit, you lose a mask. If you lose all of your masks, you die. There is no death saving throws, there is no time to potentially save someone, losing all of your health will cause you to die, making you sit out for the rest of the combat encounter, after which you will then rematerialize at the last bench you rested at.

See [[Benches]] for more info regarding what you can do at benches

The only way to increase the amount of masks you have is to explore the world and find mask shards, each shard counts as a quarter of a mask, so finding four mask shards will allow you to craft a mask on the spot and add it to your health pool

# SOUL
Your soul is your very being, you use it to heal, you use it for magic spells learnt through whatever means, it is also finite, you cannot consistently use it for your magic, you need to recover it too.

Your SOUL meter's max capacity depends on your race, but typically you will have a max capacity of 6, each hit with a melee or ranged weapon will add one to your SOUL capacity.

When you have two or more SOUL, you may perform one of the following actions:

- Heal, use 2 SOUL to fix your body and heal 1 mask
- Cast a spell, use 2 SOUL to cast a basic spell learnt
- Racial Ability, Use X SOUL (where X is the cost specified in your race) to use your ability

When you die, your soul's max capacity is reduced to 2/3rd's of what its normal max capacity would be.

> Example: If Person A's max soul capacity is 6 and they die, when they rematerialize at a bench, for as long as they dont collect their shade, their max capacity is reduced to 4.

Your SOUL capacity and gain can be improved in two ways:

- Obtain a charm
- Obtain a vessel container
