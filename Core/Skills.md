#Core-Mechanics 
There are various skills each person has that generally shows the difference between one person from another, although there is not too many skills.


| Skill         | Description                                                                                            |
| ------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Athletics     | Perform a physical feat like climbing onto something, jumping onto something, running and swimming     |
| Acrobatics    | Stay on your feet, balance your wings or move across something thin                                    |
| Deft Hands    | Perform a delicate or precise task                                                                     |
| Crafting      | Create something, repair something, or modify a tool.                                                  |
| Endurance     | Resist a poisonous or toxic substance                                                                  |
| History       | Recall your knowledge of any past events, legends or potential cultures                                |
| Perception    | Become aware of something through one of your senses.                                                  |
| Investigation | Search for clues, interpret something and uncover information that is hidden                           |
| Performance   | Put on a show, entertain through music, acting or storytelling                                         |
| Persuasion    | Persuade something to do something for you, or tell someone a lie while convincing them it's the truth |
| Survival      | Tracking creatures, enduring hostile environments etc                                                  |

When making any check with a skill, you roll a dice and add an additional dice for every point in the associated attribute, you then pick the highest value amongst your rolled dice

Below is a table for the general success-requirements and what happens on each value

| Value | Result                                                                                                |
| ----- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1-3   | A bad fail, you fumbled up somewhere and there are consequences for failing..                         |
| 4-5   | A partial success, you managed to get the job done, but theres a minor consequence associated with it |
| 6     | A full success, everything goes as planned                                                            |
| 6+6   | A critical success, you managed to go above and beyond and you might get an additional bonus          |
> For clarification: 6+6 means if you roll a 6 but on one of your other dice is also a 6, it counts as a critical success
