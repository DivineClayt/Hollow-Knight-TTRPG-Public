#Core-Mechanics 
There are two types of resources throughout the game, Geo and Shards

Geo is functionally money, you need Geo to buy things from shopkeepers, and there is a lot of ways to obtain geo, but prices in Hallownest are obviously affected by local inflation, so prices are quite high for some items.

A party may choose to pool all of their geo into one pile and collectively agree when they want to buy something, for the most part, all purchases are singular, meaning you only buy one copy of an item when you purchase it, and only some items will have multiples (story-required progressive items when obtained will be distributed to all players, and story-required progressive items that need to be bought will account for the amount of players and adjust the pricing accordingly)

> Example: a lantern that costs 1800 for 1 person, for 5 people will cost (1800 x 5), this is why collective resources is advised for your party.

Refer to [[Starting Equipment Shop]] for what you can buy from the start when creating your character and refer to your corresponding race under the characters folder for what your starting geo is.


Shards function as a resource for your tools, you need shards to create your tools, you can obtain shards by mining and by killing enemies, which provide a total amount of shards you distribute among everyone.
