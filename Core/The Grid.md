The grid is the mat the players play on, when setting up, you should just have a square-board or square-based grid, and everyone's characters should be placed at the bottom of the grid. Instead of a typical TTRPG, think of this as viewing the game from a side-perspective in a 2D manner, rather than an over the top perspective. 

In platforming segments, they will be drawn out on the grid to represent their existence, if it is a core-platforming segment, you must make it so all players are able to do it, provided they have the tools at the time required to complete it, and that no attributes or stats prevent them from being able to do so.
